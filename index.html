About ICMAB

The Institute of Cost and Management Accountants of Bangladesh (ICMAB) is the national body of professional Cost and Management Accountants established with the prime objective of promoting and regulating the CMA profession. ICMAB is a statutory body under the Ministry of Commerce, set up in 1958.

To be the leading professional body in Bangladesh for Cost and Management Accounting, recognized globally for excellence in education, research, and professional practice — empowering members to drive sustainable economic growth.

To develop competent Cost and Management Accountants through quality education and training, uphold professional ethics and standards, and contribute to national development by providing expert financial and management advisory services.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission fee for the CMA Course?

The admission fee for the CMA course is Tk. 27,000/-. Other fees include registration, tuition, and examination fees. Please visit the Fees & Charges section for a complete breakdown.

Does ICMAB solely offer Cost and Management Accountant (CMA) certifications?

Yes, ICMAB is the sole authority that offers and confers the Cost and Management Accountant (CMA) certification in Bangladesh. No other body can legally offer this designation in the country.

Is ICMAB a government organization?

ICMAB is a statutory organization constituted by the Government of Bangladesh and administered under the Ministry of Commerce, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

Is CMA a government degree?

No. CMA is a professional certification — not a government academic degree. However, it is legally recognized both locally in Bangladesh and globally by international accounting bodies like IFAC.

Is it possible to complete the CMA course alongside a full-time job?

Yes, the correspondence/online system allows students to complete the CMA course alongside a full-time job. Classes are held during evenings and weekends, and a fully digital e-learning platform is available.

What are the minimum qualifications to enroll in the CMA program?

Minimum qualification is HSC (Higher Secondary Certificate) or equivalent. However, graduates from any discipline are also welcome to apply. Please refer to the CMA Entry guidelines for complete details.
